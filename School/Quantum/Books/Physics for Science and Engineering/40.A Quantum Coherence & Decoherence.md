## Key Idea
Quantum **decoherence** is the loss of quantum **coherence**.

We can think of quantum decoherence as a factor that turns a quantum system into a system that can be understood using **classical mechanics**.

Is similar to **entanglement**, because is one of those important quantum features.

## Quantum Decoherence
### Introduction
The **interaction of the environment with the quantum system**. This environment interferes with the system, gains information.

It is inevitable for decoherence to take place, because there is no **isolated quantum system**.

The leaking out of **quantum coherence**.

### Decoherence Time 
A **Rabi Cycle** is the cyclic behavior of a two-level (two possible energy levels) quantum system when in the presence of an oscillatory driving field. For example, let a particle be excited and not excited. If we add an *oscillatory driving field*, we'll se an **alternation** between both states. [See here!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Quantum_superposition_of_states_and_decoherence.ogv)

As we saw, **isolation is impossible** thus, this oscillations will eventually stop which means **no superposition** and **loss of quantum coherence**.

The **time** that takes for the superposition to disappear is called **decoherence time**.
