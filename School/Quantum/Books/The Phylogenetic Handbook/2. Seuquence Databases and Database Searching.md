## 2.1 Introduction
Sequence data is stored in **digital databases**. You can search sequences by id, keyword or even similarity.

## 2.2 Sequence Databases
### 2.2.1 General Nucleic Acid Sequence Databases
The main databases are:
- European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL).
- GenBank
- DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ)

They **cross-submit** all their data. By convention, the sequences are of DNA.

Every sequence has the following parameters:
- **Entry name, locus name or id (ID):** The identifier of the sequence.
- **Accession Number (AC):** It is constant across all databases. There is a *primary* and *secondary*.
- **Version Number:** The AC + increment.
- **GeneInfo Number (GeneBank only):** A unique number given by the NCBI (the admins of GeneBank).
- **Whole Genome Shotgun (WGS) sequences:** Sequences from genomes obtained via the *shotgun* method.
