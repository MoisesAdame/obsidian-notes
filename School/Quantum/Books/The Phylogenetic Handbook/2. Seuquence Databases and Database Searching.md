## 2.1 Introduction
Sequence data is stored in **digital databases**. You can search sequences by id, keyword or even similarity.

## 2.2 Sequence Databases
### 2.2.1 General Nucleic Acid Sequence Databases
The main databases are:
- European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL).
- GenBank
- DNA Data Bank of Japan (DDBJ)

They **cross-submit** all their data. By convention, the sequences are of DNA.
