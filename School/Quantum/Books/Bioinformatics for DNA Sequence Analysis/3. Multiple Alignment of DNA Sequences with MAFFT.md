## 1. Introduction
In this chapter the MSA algorithms of **MAFFT**, **TBA** and **MAUVE** are explained. The fist one is very good, but assumes that the *order of aligned sites* is consistent. While the last two are *specialized for genome alignment*,

### 1.1 Basic Concepts
**Sequence alignment** is set of corresponding residues. The **interpretation** is that they have same *origin* and/or *functionality*.

### 1.2 MSA Strategies in MAFFT
#### 1.2.1 Progressive Method
This is the most widely used method, starts off with a *guide tree*. In the case of **MAFFT** it uses a *two cycle progressive method*, which is a progressive method, but repeated twice, called **FFT-NS-2**. In the other hand **FFT-NS-1** is the same but has one cycle, thus is *less accurate*.

The drawback of the progressive method is that if there are *incorrectly introduced gaps*, the gap is never removed. Although there are methods that improve accuracy. **Iterative refinement** and **consistency-based** methods.

#### 1.2.2 Iterative Refinement Method
It works by defining the **"goodness"** of the alignment and maximizing it. There have been many proposed functions being **WPS** the most successful, used in **FFT-NS-i** of MAFFT.

#### 1.2.3 Consistency-Based Iterative Refinement Method
The grading is based on **how consistent is on pairwise-alignments** and there have been many criteria proposed, being the most successful **TCOFFEE**.
