## 1.1 Mutational Origination as an Evolutionary Cause
### Generalities
Mutations and Evolution:
- Mutation tendencies result in patterns that allow the prediction of evolution.
- Patterns in evolution may have mutational explanations.

**Evolutionary Biology:** The science of evolutionary causes and effects.

### Origin-Fixation Models
An **origin-fixation model** is a model that studies evolution trough two steps
1. **Origin** of new mutations.
2. **Fixation** of those mutations.

The **total rate of evolution ($r$)** is the sum of origin-fixation rates for all possible mutations, for instance:
$$
r = \sum_{i}^{n} \mu_{ik} N \pi_{ik}
$$
Where:
- $n$: Is the number of mutations.
- $\mu$: Is the origin rate.
- $N$: The population size.
- $\pi$: The probability of fixation.
- $i$: The parental genotype.

If we want the **probability of a given mutation ($p_{j}$)** we say:
$$
p_{j} = \frac{\mu_{ik} \pi_{ik}}{\sum_{i}^{n} \mu_{ik} \pi_{ik}}
$$

### Kinetic Advantage
Not all mutations act the same. Some are more probable due to the number of ways we can reach them. For instance
- Met → Ile can be achieved: (ATG → ATT,ATG → ATC and ATG → ATA).
- Met → Val can be achieved: (ATG → GTG).

This makes **Met → Ile** more common.

This is makes the Darwinian saying that **"that the fittest survives"**, have exceptions that rely on **chance**, **contingency**, and **constraints**.
