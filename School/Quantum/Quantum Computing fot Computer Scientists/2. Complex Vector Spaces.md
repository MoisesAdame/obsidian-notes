## 2.1 $\mathrm{C}^{n}$ as the Primary Example
### Addition and Subtraction
Addition and subtraction in a **complex vector space** is both commutative and associative. Additionally its identity is the zero vector $\vec{0}$.

Let $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$ be:
$$
\vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix}
6 - 4i \\
7 + 3i \\
4.2 - 8.1i \\
-3i \\
\end{bmatrix},
\vec{w} = \begin{bmatrix}
16 +. 2.3i \\
-7i \\
6 \\
-4i \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Their addition is:
$$
\vec{v} + \vec{w} = \begin{bmatrix}
22 - 1.7i \\
7 - 4i \\
10.2 - 8.1i \\
-7i \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Scalar Multiplication
It is formally defined as the following: $z \vec{v} = zv[j]$. Additionally is **distributive** over addition.

Let $z = 8 - 2i$ be a scalar. Thus, the result would be:
$$
z \vec{w} = \begin{bmatrix}
132.6 - 13.6i \\
70 \\
48 -12i \\
32 -42i \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$
