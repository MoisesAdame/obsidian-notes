## HSK2
### 1. 离
我学校**离**我家非常远。(My is school is very far from my house.)

### 2. 还和还没
我已经有我的机票，但是她们都**还没**有。(I already have my flight tickets, but they don't have them yet.)

### 3. 呢
我的裤子**呢**？(And my pants?)

### 4. 从。。。到。。。
**从**星期一**到**星期五，我们上课。 (From Monday, to Friday we attend our classes.)

### 5. 结果补语
她**吃干净**了，我还没开始吃饭。(She has eaten all her food, I haven't started to eat yet.)
因为我忘记带我眼镜，所以我**没看清楚**。(Because I forgot to bring my glasses, I couldn't see clearly).
这个礼物是一个朋友**送给**我的。(This is a present given to me by a friend.)
我**听懂**了。(I heard and understood.)

### 6. 不要/别
**别**着急，你学会了语法。(Don't panic, you dominate all the grammar. )

### 7. 得
我妈妈画**得**特别美。(My mom draws specially beautifully.)
我姐姐做饭做**得**很好吃。(My sister cooks very delicious.)
### 8. 过和没有。。。过
我还**没有**去**过**中国。(I haven't been to China yet.)
我练习**过**打拳击三次。(I have practiced boxing three times.)

### 9. 都。。。了
**都**早上九点半**了**，该离开。(It's already 9:30 a.m., time to go.)

### 10. 虽然。。。但是。。。
**虽然**他很穷，**但是**他毕业了。
