## 1. 证明AR（1）是个二阶宽平稳过程
AR（1）是
$$
Y_{t} = \phi Y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_{t}
$$
展开一下
$$
Y_{t} = \varepsilon_{t} + \phi\varepsilon_{t-1} + \phi^2\varepsilon_{t-2} + \phi^3\varepsilon_{t-3} + \cdots
$$

重要的提醒（白噪声的分布是正态分布）
$$
\varepsilon_{t} \sim N(0, \sigma^2)
$$


算*希望值*
$$
\begin{align}
\mathbb{E}[Y_{t}] & = \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_{t} + \phi\varepsilon_{t-1} + \phi^2\varepsilon_{t-2} + \phi^3\varepsilon_{t-3} + \cdots] \\
& = \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_{t}] + \mathbb{E}[\phi\varepsilon_{t-1}] + \mathbb{E}[\phi^2\varepsilon_{t-2}] + \mathbb{E}[\phi^3\varepsilon_{t-3}] + \cdots \\
& = 0 \quad \text{（因为白噪声的均值是）}
\end{align}
$$

算*方差*
$$
\begin{align}
Var[Y_{t}]&=Var[\varepsilon_{t} + \phi\varepsilon_{t-1} + \phi^2\varepsilon_{t-2} + \phi^3\varepsilon_{t-3} + \cdots] \\
& = Var[\varepsilon_{t}] + Var[\phi\varepsilon_{t-1}] + Var[\phi^2\varepsilon_{t-2}] + Var[\phi^3\varepsilon_{t-3}] + \cdots \\
& = Var[\varepsilon_{t}] + \phi^2Var[\varepsilon_{t-1}] + \phi^4Var[\varepsilon_{t-2}] + \phi^6Var[\varepsilon_{t-3}] + \cdots \\
& = \sigma^2 + \phi^2\sigma^2 + \phi^4\sigma^2 + \phi^6\sigma^2 + \cdots \\
& = \sigma^2 (1 + \phi^2 + \phi^4 + \phi^6 + \cdots) \\
& =\frac{\sigma^2}{1 - \phi ^2}
\end{align}
$$

算**协方差**
第一选择（$t_{1}>t_{2}$）
$$
\begin{align}
\gamma(t_{1}, t_{2})&=Cov[Y_{t_{1}}, Y_{t_{2}}] \\
&=Cov[\varepsilon_{t_{1}} + \phi\varepsilon_{t_{1}-1} + \cdots,\varepsilon_{t_{2}} + \phi\varepsilon_{t_{2}-1} + \cdots ] \\
& =Cov[\varepsilon_{t_{1}} + \phi\varepsilon_{t_{1}-1} + \cdots +\phi^{t_{1}-t_{2}}\varepsilon_{t_{2}} + \cdots,\varepsilon_{t_{2}}+ \phi\varepsilon_{t_{2}-1} + \cdots ] \quad (\text{因为 }t_{1}>t_{2})  \\
& =Cov[\varepsilon_{t_{1}} + \phi\varepsilon_{t_{1}-1} + \cdots +\phi^{t_{1}-t_{2}}Y_{t_{2}},Y_{t_{2}} ]  \\
&=Cov[\varepsilon_{t_{1}},Y_{t_{2}} ]  + Cov[\phi\varepsilon_{t_{1}-1},Y_{t_{2}} ]  + \cdots +Cov[\phi^{t_{1}-t_{2}}Y_{t_{2}},Y_{t_{2}} ] \quad \text{因为 }Cov[X+Y, Z] = Cov[X, Z] + Cov[Y, Z]\\
& = 0  + 0  + \cdots +0+Cov[\phi^{t_{1}-t_{2}}Y_{t_{2}},Y_{t_{2}} ]\quad (\varepsilon \text{和} Y_{t_{2}}\text{是完全独立的})\\
& = \phi^{t_{1}-t_{2}}\mathbb{E}[(Y_{t_{2}})^2] \\
& = \phi^{t_{1}-t_{2}}Var[Y_{t_{2}}] \\
& = \phi^{t_{1}-t_{2}} \frac{\sigma^2}{1-\phi}
\end{align}
$$

第一选择（$t_{2}>t_{1}$）
$$
\begin{align}
\gamma(t_{1}, t_{2})&=Cov[Y_{t_{1}}, Y_{t_{2}}] \\
& = \phi^{t_{2}-t_{1}} \frac{\sigma^2}{1-\phi}
\end{align}
$$

既然**希望值**和**方差**都是常量，而且自协方差仅仅依赖于$t_{1}-t_{2}$所以AR(1)过程是个**二阶宽平稳过程**。

## 2. 平稳条件下的均值是无偏的
要证明：
$$
\mu = \mathbb{E}[\hat{\mu}]
$$

知道$\hat{y}$是样本的均值
$$
\hat{y} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^ny_{i}
$$

在我们条件下，因为在分析个平稳过程，所以$y_{i} = Y_{i}$
$$
\mathbb{E}[\hat{y}] = \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^nY_{i}\right]
$$
$$
\mathbb{E}[\hat{y}] = \frac{1}{n}\mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{i=1}^nY_{i}\right]
$$
$$
\mathbb{E}[\hat{y}] = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb{E}\left[Y_{i}\right]
$$
$$
\mathbb{E}[\hat{y}] = \frac{1}{n}n\mu=\mu
$$

## 3. 平稳条件下的方差近似
要证明
$$
Var[\hat{\mu}]=\frac{1}{n^2}\sum_{t=1}^n\sum_{s=1}^n\gamma(t-s)
$$

方差的原来公式是
$$
Var[Y(t)] = \mathbb{E}[(Y(t) - \mu(t))^2]
$$

因此
$$
Var[\hat{\mu}]=Var\left[ \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i =1}^ny_{i}\right] = \mathbb{E}\left[\left(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i =1}^ny_{i} -\mu\right)^2\right]
$$

因为$Var[cX]=c^2Var[X]$
$$
Var[\hat{\mu}]=\frac{1}{n^2}Var\left[\sum_{i =1}^ny_{i}\right]
$$

因为$Var\left[ \sum_{t} X \right]=Cov\left[ \sum_{t}X, \sum_{s} X\right]$
$$
Var[\hat{\mu}]=\frac{1}{n^2}Cov\left[ \sum_{i=1}^n X_{i}, \sum_{j=1}^n X_{j} \right] = \frac{1}{n^2} \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n Cov\left[X_{i},  X_{j} \right] = \frac{1}{n^2} \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n \gamma(i-j)
$$
