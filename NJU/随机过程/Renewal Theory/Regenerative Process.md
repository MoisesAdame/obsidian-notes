## Definition
Considering an stochastic process $\{ X(t), t\geq 0 \}$ who has the property of having points $T_{1}, T_{2},\dots$, where the whole process restarts probabilistically, that is a **regenerative process**.

---
## Proposition 1
On the long run, the proportion of time in an state $\{ X(t) = j\}$ is:
$$
\text{proportion of time in state j} = \frac{\mathbb{E}[\text{time of j in a given cycle}]}{\mathbb{E}[\text{duration of that cycle}]}
$$
