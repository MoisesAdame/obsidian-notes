## Definition
Let $Y_{1}, Y_{2}, \dots$ be independent and identically distributed with:
$$
P\{ Y_{i} = 1\} = P\{ Y_{i} = -1\} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

And define
$$
Z_{0}, \quad Z_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^nY_{i}
$$

The process $\{ Z_{n}, n\geq1 \}$ is a **symmetric random walk** process.
