## Definition
A Hilbert space is a complete inner product vector space.

## Properties
### The Riesz Representation Theorem
For a continuous linear functional $\phi$ on a Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$, there exists a unique $u \in \mathcal{H}$ such that $\phi(v) = \langle u, v\rangle$ for all $v \in \mathcal{H}$.
