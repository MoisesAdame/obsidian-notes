## Definition 
A normed vector space $V$ is a vector space who has defined a norm $\| \cdot  \| : V \to \mathbb{R}$ and satisfies the following axioms for all $\vec{x}, \vec{y} \in V$ and $a \in \mathbb{R}$
1. $\|\vec{x}\| \geq 0$
2. $\|\vec{x} + \vec{y}\| \leq \|\vec{x}\| + \| \vec{y}\|$
3. $\|a\vec{x}\| = \lvert a \rvert \cdot \|\vec{x}\|$
