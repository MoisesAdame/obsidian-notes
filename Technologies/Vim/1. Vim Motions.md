## Normal Mode

### Simple Navigation

- Navigate Up: `k`
- Navigate Down: `j` 
- Navigate Left: `h`
- Navigate Right: `l`

### Motion Inside Lines

- Jump Beginning of Line: `$` 
- Jump End of Line: `0` 
- Jump to a given letter: `f + <letter>`
 
### Motion Between Lines

- Move Sentence Up: `(`
- Move Sentence Up: `)`
- Move Paragraph Up: `{`
- Move Paragraph Up: `}`
- Move to the Top of the Page: `gg`
- Move to the Bottom of the Page: `GG`

## Insert Mode

### Where to Insert

- Between the cursor: `i`
- Beginning of the Line: `I`
- End of the Line: `A`
- Before Current Line: `O` 
- After Current Line: `o`

### Copy and Paste

- Copy: `yy`
- Paste: `p` 

### Undo and Redo

- Undo: `u`
- Redo: `ctrl + r` (Not Proven)

### Delete Things

- Delete Word: `d + w`
- Delete Sentence: `d + s`
- Delete Paragraph: `d + p`
- Delete a Whole Line: `dd`
