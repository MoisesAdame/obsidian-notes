## 1.3 Cars And Goats: The Monty Hall Dilema
You are offered to open a door, in a selection of three doors. two of them have goats and one has a car. Fist, one is opened with a goat. Now, what would be the best decision? To change or not to change? In fact you should switch because initially the door you picked had a $\frac{1}{3}$ probability of having the car, while the two doors you didn't pick had a $\frac{2}{3}$ probability of having the car. When Monty opens the door in the second set, it still has the $\frac{2}{3}$ probability, while yours has $\frac{1}{3}$.
