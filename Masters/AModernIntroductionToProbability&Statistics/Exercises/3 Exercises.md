##### Quick Exercise 3.1 Let $N = R^{C}$ What is the conditional probability of $P(N | L)$

$$
N = R^{C} = \{ May, Jun, Jul, Aug \}
$$
Thus, following the conditional probability formula, we get:
$$
P(N | L) = \frac{P(N \cap L)}{P(L)} = \frac{}{}
