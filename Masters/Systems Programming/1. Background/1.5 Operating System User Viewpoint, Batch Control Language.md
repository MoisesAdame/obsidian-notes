## The Monitor System
- **Monitor:** Are the control programs (the ones in char of errors, secondary memory, I/O, etc.) of the OS.
- **Batch System:** Processes each job.
- The monitor *processes* those jobs.

## Control Language
- This is how we communicate in a **batch monitor system**.
- There are two types of cards: Execution and definition.
	- First we **define** a job.
	- Then we **execute** the steps (programs) that belong to it.
- We have to **execute** things such as the loader, and the objects (our programs).
