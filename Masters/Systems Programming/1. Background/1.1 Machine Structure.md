## What are Systems Programs

- Programs are responsible for the **development** and **execution** of a program.
- They are software like compilers, loaders, operating systems and macro processors.
	- **Compilers:** Understand *human language* and take it to *machine language*.
	- **Loaders:** System programs that prepare *machine language* for execution.
	- **Macro Processors:** Allow programmers to use abbreviations.
	- **Operating Systems:** Flexible storage and retrieval of information.

## Definitions

- **Memory:** Where the info is stored.
- **Processor:** Who performs operations given by memory instructions.
- **Addresses:** The locations of the memory.
- **Program:** A Sequence of instructions.

## Processors

- There are 2 types: I/O and CPU.
- Input / Output Processors:
	- Transfer data between memory and peripherals.
	- Activated by the CPU.
- CPU:
	- Manipulation of data stored in memory.
