## States And Transitions

- **State:** Is an instantaneous description of a system.
- **Transition:** Is a change of state.
- **Finite state and transition system:** Is a system which has a finite amount of states and transitions. These are modeled through a **finite automaton**.

## Finite Automata

- It's formal name is a *deterministic finite automaton (DFA)*.
$$
M= ( Q, \Sigma, \delta, s, F)
$$
- Where:
	- **States ( $Q$ ):** A finite set of states.
	- **Input Alphabet ( $\sum$ ):** Finite set of characters.
	- **Transition Function ( $\delta$ ):** Tells us which state to move, given some input. Expressed as a relation like $Q \times \sum \rightarrow Q$ 
	- **Start State ( $s$ ):** The state where we enter. $s \in Q$.
	- **Final States ( $F$  ):** The states where the automaton ends. $F\subset Q$.

- Not all strings are accepted and thats where the notion of **language accepted by $M$** comes into play. Its is defined as the following:
- Let:
$$
 \widehat{\delta}: Q \times \Sigma^* \rightarrow Q
$$
- Then:
$$
L(M)=\{x \in \Sigma^* | \widehat{\delta}(s, x) \in F \}
$$
- This is what we call a **regular set** and is $A = L(M) \subset \Sigma^*$.
